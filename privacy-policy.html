Privacy policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Developer: YANGZstudios

About this policy

Anime Block Burst is an Android block puzzle game developed by YANGZstudios. This policy describes the current development version, including Stage Mode, Free Mode, local guest saves and cosmetic previews. Contact yangzstudios@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Game data on your device

The game stores stage completions, collected pictures, the active stage and Free Mode runs, scores, board and piece state, picture reveal, Continue state, cosmetic selections, sound and motion preferences, guest choice and save-recovery copies in this app's local storage. It creates a random installation identifier and local diagnostic records of gameplay, advertising and errors. This version does not upload saved games or local gameplay logs to a server operated by YANGZstudios.

No player account is required. Continue as Guest uses local storage. Google and Apple sign-in and cloud backup are not enabled; Coming soon controls do not sign you in. The app does not ask for contacts, photos, microphone or precise device location.

Advertising and Google AdMob

The game integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded Continue ads and interstitial ads between runs. Google may process advertising/device identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device/app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertising, limit frequency and prevent fraud. Google processes that information under its own policies.

See Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and its explanation of partner services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Development testing uses sample ads or registered test devices.

Consent and your advertising choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and present applicable consent or privacy choices. Advertising requests are gated on the SDK's CanRequestAds result. When Google indicates that privacy options are required, an Ad privacy choices control is available from the in-app privacy page and the How to play screen.

You can also manage advertising identifiers and personalization in Android's privacy or Google advertising settings. Available options depend on the device and region. Core puzzle play is available offline; network advertising is unavailable without a connection.

Collections and purchases

Pictures are earned by completing the configurable target of each of 13 sequential stages. Each 10% of a stage's target reveals another picture strip. Completing Stage 1 unlocks Free Mode, which uses only owned pictures and does not award new campaign pictures. Pictures, records and stage attempts remain separate local records.

Default Slate Minimal is free. Optional complete theme packs contain a board, animated blocks, clearing/combo effects and sounds. Clearing Stage 10 earns the Night Arcade board and animated blocks and records a permanent local completion offer for the remaining effects and sounds. Merely reaching Stage 10 grants no reward. This milestone and its offer are local guest progress; they are not currently synchronized between devices.

Real-money checkout is disabled in the current development build. Previewing or applying earned content creates no payment transaction. The Google Play Billing integration is present for the planned Android purchases. When checkout is enabled, Google Play will handle payment and may process purchase/account and transaction information under Google's policies. YANGZstudios does not receive payment-card details from this integration.

When purchases are enabled and verified, the app stores product identifiers and signed Google Play receipt data locally to validate cosmetic ownership, remember selections and support purchase restoration. Purchase/restoration requests go through Google Play; no developer-operated purchase verification server is configured in this version. Store purchase restoration is separate from game sign-in and local campaign backup. It requires the appropriate Google Play account and a valid store entitlement. Restoring purchases does not restore campaign scores or an unpurchased Stage 10 milestone.

Retention, deletion and security

Local guest progress and preferences can be lost when you clear app storage, uninstall the game or change devices. Device/system backups, if enabled, are managed by Android and your device provider; this game does not provide a cloud-save recovery guarantee. The local guest-choice and Account & Backup screens explain this limitation.

Locally stored receipts and cosmetic preferences may also be removed with app storage. Where store purchases are enabled, verified entitlements can be restored separately using Google Play. To manage Google's account, advertising or transaction data, use Google's controls and privacy information. For questions about data processed by YANGZstudios, contact yangzstudios@gmail.com.

We do not sell personal information. Google controls retention of information it processes under its policies. The app uses checksummed saves, separate ownership records and backup recovery to reduce accidental local data loss; no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact yangzstudios@gmail.com so we can review the request.
