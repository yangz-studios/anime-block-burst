Privacy policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Developer: YANGZstudios

About this policy

Anime Block Burst is an Android block puzzle game developed by YANGZstudios. This policy describes the current development version and how it handles information. Contact us at yangzstudios@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Game data on your device

The game stores your current puzzle, score, personal best, sound preferences, and save-recovery copies in its own app storage. It also creates a random installation identifier and local diagnostic records of game sessions and advertising events. These records help us test gameplay, save recovery and ad behavior. This version does not send your saved game or local gameplay logs to a server operated by YANGZstudios.

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, microphone, precise device location or other personal files. No player account is required.

Advertising and Google AdMob

The game integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded Continue ads and interstitial ads between runs. Google may process advertising/device identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device/app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertising, limit frequency and prevent fraud. Google processes that information under its own policies.

See Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and its explanation of partner services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Development testing uses sample ads or registered test devices.

Consent and your advertising choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and present applicable consent or privacy choices. Advertising requests are gated on the SDK's CanRequestAds result. When Google indicates that privacy options are required, an Ad privacy choices control is available from the in-app privacy page and the How to play screen.

You can also manage advertising identifiers and personalization in Android's privacy or Google advertising settings. Available options depend on the device and region. Core puzzle play is available offline; network advertising is unavailable without a connection.

Collections and purchases

Picture Collections and real-money cosmetic purchases are not enabled in the current development version. Before those features are introduced, this policy and the app's data disclosures will be updated to describe ownership storage, purchase verification, restoration and any additional service providers.

Retention, deletion and security

Local game data remains in this app's storage until it is replaced by normal gameplay, or removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Device/system backups, if enabled, are managed by Android and your device provider. Clearing app storage resets this installation's local progress and settings.

We do not sell personal information. The advertising processing described above is performed by Google. Google controls retention of information it processes under its policies. The app uses checksummed saves and backup recovery to reduce accidental local data loss; no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact yangzstudios@gmail.com so we can review the request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The effective date identifies the current policy. Developer: YANGZstudios. Privacy and support contact: yangzstudios@gmail.com.
