Anime Block Burst

Make space. Reveal something beautiful.

An upcoming Android block puzzle about earning your way to a collection of anime artwork.

Privacy policyGet in touch

Puzzle your way to a picture

Place blocks, clear lines and build your score. The picture reveal follows your run: 1,000 points reveals 10%, and 10,000 completes the artwork and adds it permanently to COLLECTIONS.

The game is in development. The first development build includes picture Collections and default backgrounds. Cosmetic purchases are planned; no public store release is linked here yet.
