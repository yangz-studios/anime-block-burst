Anime Block Burst

Place blocks. Reveal pictures. Build your collection.

Stage Mode

Play 13 sequential picture stages. Each stage has its own score target; every 10% of that target reveals another horizontal strip. Complete the stage to keep its picture in Collections, unlock the next stage, then choose Keep Playing or Next Stage.

Free Mode

Complete Stage 1 to unlock untimed endless play with your owned pictures, fully revealed. Free Mode records are separate and it never awards campaign pictures.

Your look

Slate Minimal is included free. The planned Night Arcade, Gallery and Sakura Atelier complete packs each include a board, animated blocks, clearing/combo and sounds. My Look lets you mix owned components.

Complete packs have an agreed US base price of $4.99 each. Clearing Stage 10 earns the Night Arcade board and animated blocks, with a permanent local $1.99 offer for the remaining clearing/combo and sounds. These are development catalogue prices; live checkout is currently disabled. Localized store prices will be shown when purchases are enabled.

Guest saves and backup

Guest progress is stored on your device and can be lost on uninstall, clearing app storage or device change. No separate game login or campaign cloud backup is planned for this launch. When purchases are enabled, paid packs can be recovered with the original Google Play account through Restore Purchases. Campaign progress and earned rewards remain local, and purchases do not transfer between Google Play and the App Store.
